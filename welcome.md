# Welcome to My Blog

Hi there! This is the first post on my new static blog.

I built this site using plain **HTML**, **CSS**, and **Markdown** — no frameworks,
no build tools, just simple files that are easy to understand and easy to extend.

## Why static?

- Fast to load
- Easy to host (GitHub Pages, Netlify, or even just a folder)
- No database or backend required
- Content lives in readable `.md` files
